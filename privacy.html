Privacy Policy

FaceAnonymizer · Last updated 22 October 2026

FaceAnonymizer collects no data. The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It contains no networking code.

Your photos

Photos are processed entirely on your iPhone. When you choose a photo, the system photo picker gives the app only that one photo; the app cannot see the rest of your library. Photos you take with the camera inside the app are kept in memory only and are never saved without stickers.

Face detection uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Nothing is uploaded.

What the app saves

When you tap Save, the app adds the anonymized image to your photo library as a new photo. For this it asks for "Add Photos Only" access, which cannot read your existing photos. The saved file contains no location, camera details or other metadata. When you share, the image goes only where you send it.

Permissions

  • Camera – only when you tap Take Photo.
  • Photos (add only) – only when you tap Save to Photos.

Children

The app is designed to help parents protect children's privacy. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.
